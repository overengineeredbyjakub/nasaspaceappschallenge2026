NASA Data & Resources

NASA Earthdata Search: Earthdata Search enables data discovery, search, comparison, visualization, and access across NASA's Earth science data holdings.

ASF Data Search Vertex: The Alaska Satellite Facility (ASF) operates the NASA archive of SAR data and provides services to researchers supporting NASA's ESDIS project.

NISAR Data User Guide: This guide from ASF aims to help those who research and monitor the Earth find all the information they need to know about accessing and working with NISAR data in one place.

Intro to Remote Sensing: Learn the basics about remotely-sensed data, from instrument characteristics to different types of resolution to data processing and analysis.

Intro to Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR): Learn the basics about SAR data, from the roles of frequency and wavelength to polarization and scattering mechanisms.

NASA ARSET Trainings: Online trainings for beginners and advanced practitioners alike, across a breadth of topics including disaster management, ecological forecasting, agriculture, public health, air quality, and water resources management. Includes introductory materials for remote sensing, SAR, and NISAR in English and Spanish.

NISAR StoryMaps: Interactive StoryMaps on SAR applications for floods, wildfires, land subsidence, oil slicks, and glacial motion.

NISAR Applications White Papers: Brief 2-page overviews of relevant applications of NISAR data that provide societal benefit as identified by past NISAR Applications Workshops.

UAVSAR Postcards: False color composites of airborne UAVSAR data depicting various Earth processes.

Space Agency Partner Resources

CSA (Canadian Space Agency)

Radarsat Constellation Mission (RCM) - Data: The RADARSAT Constellation Mission (RCM) is Canada's new generation of Earth observation satellites. The RCM includes a trio of Earth observation satellites, capable of scanning Earth day or night and in any weather conditions. The RCM is designed to provide effective solutions in three main areas: Maritime surveillance (ice, surface wind, oil pollution and ship monitoring); Disaster management (mitigation, warning, response and recovery); and Ecosystem monitoring (agriculture, wetlands, forestry and coastal change monitoring).

Synthetic Aperture Radar python Tutorial: This tutorial demonstrates how to work with SAR imagery using Python and QGIS. It includes easy and practical steps for accessing and visualizing data.

RADARSAT 1 - Data: Launched in November 1995, RADARSAT-1 provided Canada and the world with an operational radar satellite system capable of timely delivery of large amounts of data. Equipped with a powerful synthetic aperture radar (SAR) instrument, it acquired images of the Earth day or night, in all weather and through cloud cover, smoke, and haze. All data products in the Canadian RADARSAT-1 archive have been released under an open license.

National Space Activities Commission of Argentina (CONAE)

SAOCOM Catalogue: Access CONAE's latest SAR mission, SAOCOM catalogue and general information to register, download the images. Download and processing tutorials. Register for a ONE CLICK Login Profile.

SAOCOM Catalogue Registration: Registration protocol to access SAOCOM catalogue with ONE CLICK Profile.
